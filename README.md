**Sales Analysis Report using Power BI**

This project presents a sales analysis using Power BI, providing key insights into profit, units sold, and sales performance across different months, cities, beverage brands, and retailers. The analysis aims to identify trends, highlight top performers, and support data-driven decision-making.

**Key Metrics**

Profit: $3.17M
Units Sold: 17M
Total Sales: $8.68M
Operating Margin: 36.31%

**Visualizations**

Bar Chart (Total Sales by Month)
The total sales in July and December were both $1.0M, highlighting peak performance during these months.

Pie Chart (Total Sales by City)
New York City has the highest sales, contributing $391.77K to the total sales.

Pie Chart (Units Sold by Beverage Brand)
Coca-Cola leads in units sold, with a total of 4M units sold.

Bar Graph (Total Sales by Retailer)
SodaPop stands out as the retailer with the highest sales.

**Insights**

July and December were key months for sales, each achieving $1.0M in revenue.

New York City remains the top-performing city in terms of total sales.

Coca-Cola is the most sold beverage brand, followed by significant sales from other brands.

SodaPop is the leading retailer, contributing significantly to total sales.

**Conclusion**

This Power BI report provides actionable insights into the sales trends and performance by month, city, beverage brand, and retailer. The findings can assist in identifying high-performing areas and focus points for improvement in other regions or segments.
